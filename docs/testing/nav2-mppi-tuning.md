# Nav2 controller tuning for the M7 Nav2 evader

Date: 2026-10-08  
Status: finished; **revised after the red-team review (last section,
"Red-team revision")**. Several statements below were true of the earlier
configuration only (global `robot_radius` 0.22/0.30, inside-the-arena border,
`plan_start` margin 0.3) and are superseded there. Shipped controller:
**RegulatedPurePursuit (RPP)**, not MPPI.
Raw per-variant results: `artifacts/nav2-m7-tuning/` (`summary.json`, `runs/*.json`).

## Question

Acceptance target (task brief): with **no boids**, the target should drive an
obstacle course at a mean speed **>= 2.5 m/s** (cap 3.6), **without touching an
obstacle** and **without stopping**. Limits are derived from `params.yaml`, not
hard-coded: `v_max = agent_max_speed (2.0) x target_speed_multiplier (1.8) =
3.6 m/s`, `omega_max = target_omega_max = 1.2 rad/s`. Game-balance parameters
were not touched.

## Harness (`tools/nav2_tune.py`, final version "v3")

Headless `pygame_sim` (0 boids, `time_scale` 1.0, **stamina off** so the
controller's own capability is measured) + `nav2_target.launch.py` with a
candidate yaml (generated by `tools/nav2_tune_gen.py`). Goals are sent straight
to `ComputePathToPose` + `FollowPath`; a chain hands off to the next goal
(preempting `FollowPath`) when within 3 m of the current one, which is how the
evader uses the controller. Five scenarios, 2 repetitions each:

| scenario | what it is |
|---|---|
| `single_block`, `wall_with_gap`, `slalom` | the three spike courses (start-relative), one leg each |
| `diamond` | 4 waypoints with 90-degree corners, an obstacle on every edge and one in the middle; 5 legs, no reversals |
| `obstacle_field` | the game's fixed maze (`env:=obstacle_field`), a chain of 5 goals, each the farthest free point from the previous one |

Two speeds are reported (sim-time, from `/target/pose`):

- **cruise** = mean speed from the first sample with v >= 1 m/s until within
  2 m of the final goal. It excludes the initial pivot and the final stop.
- **incl. startup** = path length / time from the first `FollowPath` goal to
  arrival. It includes the initial pivot and the final stop.

Why both: the target can only turn at 1.2 rad/s, so a path that starts behind
it costs up to ~2.6 s of standing pivot (measured `time_to_first_motion` of
1.3-3.6 s for the controllers below). The spike's "1.7-1.9 m/s" was an
*incl. startup* number on a single leg.

`collided` = body within 0.02 m of an obstacle or wall. `stalled` = 2 s window
with mean speed < 0.3 m/s *and* not pivoting (< 0.3 rad/s), after first motion,
> 1 m from the goal.

### Earlier harness versions (kept for the record)

- **v1** (single legs, stall flag also fired on pivoting): MPPI base 1.76
  incl-startup, `any_stall` true; `m04_lowcost` 1.66; `m03_longhorizon` 0.53
  (target stuck, collided). Variants `m01`/`m02` did not start at all because
  the generator wrote YAML ints into double parameters (`parameter ... is of
  type {double}, setting it to {integer} is not allowed`). Generator fixed.
- **v2** (chains, but the 2nd goal was often *behind* the target, so the
  hand-off needed a 180-degree pivot and the chain measured `omega_max`, not the
  controller): MPPI base cruise 2.19; `m01_stopper` (vx_std 1.5, batch 1500,
  PathFollow 10, temperature 0.2; the combination named in the brief) cruise
  1.09, `any_stall` true -> **reproduces the "target stops" failure**;
  `m02_lowalign` (PathAlign 6, PathFollow 8, PathAngle 1) collided and never
  moved; `m03_longhorizon` (time_steps 56, vx_std 1.2, temperature 0.25)
  collided and stalled; `m04_lowcost` cruise 2.22.
  These numbers are not comparable to v3 and are not used for the decision.

remaining MPPI ideas were not pursued once RPP was ahead (MPPI was not tuned
to exhaustion).

navfn paths stay out of the inscribed zone, r01) was never applied to the MPPI
variants, which kept their yaml's costmap settings. MPPI's lower speed in the
maze may therefore partly reflect that, not the controller. The red-team
revision below re-measured MPPI on the corrected footprint for two scenarios.

## What the data say

- **MPPI** (the four valid v3 rows; not given the planner-margin fix) reaches ~3.0 m/s cruise on the open spike courses
  but only ~1.4-1.5 on `diamond` and ~1.7 in the maze. Its *incl-startup* mean
  is 1.4-1.6, below 2.5 in every variant. Lowering critic costs (m04) or
  sharpening the sampler (m07) did not help and made some chains fail to reach
  the goal. Speed-oriented changes to the path critics (m05; and v2's m01, m02,
  m03) either stalled the target or drove it into obstacles. **None of the four valid MPPI variants
  met the target** (m01-m03 are not counted: v1/v2 harness only).
- **RPP** with the curve-regulation radius set to `v_max / omega_max` (3.0 m: the
  tightest curve the target can follow at full speed, since v = omega * R) met
  cruise >= 3.2 on the spike courses and 2.5-2.7 incl-startup, but **aborted in
  the maze** (`RegulatedPurePursuitController detected collision ahead!`,
  `Controller patience exceeded`, 64 warnings in the run, m08). Cause
  (diagnosed from the log, then confirmed by the fix): navfn paths hug obstacle
  corners, i.e. pass through the *inscribed* zone of the 0.15 m body, and RPP's
  look-ahead collision check rejects them. Planning the global costmap with
  `robot_radius 0.3` (planner-side margin only; the local costmap and the
  actual body stay 0.15) removed the aborts (r01).
- r02/r03 show the collision-check parameters were *not* the lever once the
  path keeps clear (identical speeds with detection on, off, or tightened).
  r04 (faster in curves) broke the maze again; r05 (wider global inflation) is
  within noise of r01 (3.09 vs 3.05) and was not adopted, to keep the change
  minimal.

## Decision

Ship **r01** (plus the wall-start changes and the radius change in the last
section): RPP, `desired_linear_vel = v_max`,
`regulated_linear_scaling_min_radius = v_max / omega_max`,
`rotate_to_heading_angular_vel = omega_max` (all three set by
`nav2_target.launch.py` from `params.yaml`), global costmap `robot_radius` 0.3 (finally 0.22, see last section).
MPPI stays available as `config/nav2_target_mppi.yaml`
(`nav2_config:=nav2_target_mppi.yaml`).

Reason (data): r01 is the only family member that is simultaneously (a) >= 2.5
m/s on the spike courses and the diamond including startup (2.54-3.38), (b)
collision-free and stall-free in all 10 chains, and (c) completes the maze. MPPI
never reached 2.5 incl-startup in any scenario.

## Honest limits of this result

- **The maze (`obstacle_field`) stays below 2.5 m/s** (2.27 cruise, 2.22
  incl-startup with the shipped controller). Inference, not measured: its
  passages force path curvature, and with omega_max = 1.2 a curve of radius R is
  only drivable at v <= 1.2 R; the regulation radius of 3.0 m already scales
  speed down there. Making the maze faster would need a larger `omega_max`,
  which is a game-balance knob and was deliberately not touched.
- The acceptance speed is measured **without stamina** (the real game has it:
  sprinting above 2.0 m/s drains stamina at 0.25/s, regen 0.35/s;
  `target_controller` therefore caps the Nav2 command at the swarm speed unless
  a boid is within `panic_distance`). In the game the achievable mean is lower.
- These runs have **no boids**. With boids the controller sees them only as
  costmap obstacles, RPP aborts on "collision ahead", and the evader falls back
  to reactive (counted as a Nav2 failure). MPPI would likely handle moving
  obstacles more gracefully (inference); that was not tested here.
- Rows m00-r05 predate the boid-clearing scan, the range settings, the
  planner start inset and RPP collision-detection-off; the shipped config was
  re-measured separately (last section).
- Runs are single-machine, loaded (load average 12-16), wall-clock sensitive;
  expect run-to-run differences of a few 0.1 m/s.

## Wall start (found by running the real game)

First sanity runs of the evader showed 8-12 `plan_fail`s per run. The planner
log named the cause: `failed to plan from (7.53, 0.15) ...`, `(17.98, 0.42)`,
`(19.85, 1.64)`. The reactive evader likes to stand against a wall, and the sim
lets the body centre sit 0.15 m from it, but the static map's border is 0.1 m
thick and the planner-side radius is 0.3, so that pose is inside the wall's
inscribed zone and navfn refuses it. Lowering the global radius back to 0.15
(r06/r07) lets the plan succeed but then RPP's own collision check aborts at
the wall (and again in the maze), so the radius was kept at 0.3 and two other
changes were made:

1. `Nav2Evader` plans from `plan_start(...)`, the robot pose moved >= 0.4 m
   (`nav2_start_margin`) off walls and obstacle surfaces (unit tested); the
   controller still follows the path from the true pose.
2. `use_collision_detection: false` in RPP. Ablation r03 showed identical
   speed and clearance on the no-boid courses with detection on or off; with
   it on, the robot standing at a wall is in "collision ahead" forever. The sim
   blocks motion physically, clearance comes from the planner, and boids are
   handled by replanning (global costmap contains them) plus the reactive
   blend. Trade-off: RPP no longer stops itself for a boid in its arc.

## Final confirmation of the shipped yaml

The sanity benchmark (`artifacts/nav2-m7-sanity/`) showed the target stranded
in the 0.6 m pocket between the bottom wall and the maze obstacle at
(1.3, 2.7, r 2.8): with global `robot_radius` 0.30 the planner refused every
start there (up to 33 `plan_fail` in 40 requests). Rows r08/r09 above (0.22,
0.18) were measured on the same code as the 0.30 baseline (cruise mean 2.85 /
incl-startup 2.49, diamond 3.38; 0.22: 2.70 / 2.30, diamond 2.53/2.59; 0.18:
2.68 / 2.27, diamond 2.40/2.44). 0.22 was adopted: smaller radii bought no
further benefit and cost diamond speed. The pocket still strands the target in
some runs at 0.22 (2 of 5 sanity runs), so this is reduced, not solved.

Final shipped yaml (RPP, global radius 0.22, detection off, boid clearing
scan, ranges 10 m local / 20 m global, `plan_start` margin 0.3), 12 chains,
`artifacts/nav2-m7-tuning/runs/final_shipped.json`:

| scenario | cruise m/s | incl-startup m/s | reached | collided | stalled |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| single_block | 3.20 | 2.54 | yes | no | no |
| wall_with_gap | 3.21 | 2.54 | yes | no | no |
| slalom | 3.21 | 2.61 | yes | no | no |
| diamond | 2.52 | 2.58 | yes | no | no |
| obstacle_field (maze) | 2.20 | 2.10 | yes | no | no |
| wall_start | 1.86 | 1.60 | yes | no | no |

Against the brief (mean >= 2.5 m/s, no obstacle contact, no stopping):
**met** on single_block, wall_with_gap, slalom and diamond (cruise 2.5-3.2,
incl-startup 2.5-2.6, diamond borderline) with no obstacle contact and no
stall in any of the 12 chains; **not met** in the maze (2.20 cruise / 2.10
incl-startup) and from a standing start against a wall (1.86 / 1.60), for the
reasons above (turn-rate bound, pivot from the wall). The all-scenario mean
(2.70 cruise, 2.30 incl-startup) is pulled down by those two. The
shipped speeds were measured without stamina; the earlier-radius (0.30)
configuration was faster on `diamond` (3.38), which is the price of the
pocket fix.

## Red-team revision (2026-10-08, after review)

The review found that the "pocket" strandings and the wall-start failures were
**one parameter inconsistency**, not bad luck: the planner footprint was bigger
than the body, the wall was drawn inside the arena, and the planner start inset
was smaller than wall + radius. Fixes and what was measured:

### Footprint / wall consistency (A)

| quantity | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| sim: body centre may be this close to a wall / obstacle surface | 0.15 m (`target_body_radius`) | same |
| global costmap `robot_radius` | 0.22 (literal in yaml) | = `target_body_radius`, written by `nav2_target.launch.py` into **both** costmaps from `params.yaml` (yaml literal is a placeholder; a test checks they agree) |
| local costmap `robot_radius` | 0.15 (literal) | same derivation |
| map border | `rasterize_circles(border_cells=1)`: lethal band **inside** the arena, 0.1 m wide (the library default 2 = 0.2 m was not what the bridge used) | `rasterize_arena`: grid padded by 1 cell, wall drawn **outside** the arena, lethal band starts exactly at the real wall; map is 202x202, origin (-0.1, -0.1) |
| cost of the legal wall-hugging cell (centre 0.15 m off the wall) | inscribed (253): navfn refuses to plan from it | ~203 (high, traversable); unit-tested against a Python copy of the InflationLayer rule and checked in the live global costmap |
| planner start | geometric inset 0.3 m < border + radius | nearest traversable cell of the evader's own copy of the cost grid (`EscapeMap.plan_start`); geometric fallback margin = radius + half a cell |

A limit that remains and is honest to state: at 0.1 m resolution a body
touching an **obstacle** is often an inscribed cell too (3558 of 4800 sampled
touching positions on the six game mazes with the conservative circle
rasteriser; 1260 of 4800 with a cell-centre rasteriser). The start is moved to
a traversable cell instead; making that exact would need a finer map
(0.05 m), which was not tried.

Pocket start of sanity seed 3, raw sim position (3.23, 0.31): before, the
sanity run logged 25 `Failed to create plan with tolerance of 0.5` in that
seed (most from this start, per the planner log); after, `test_wall_hugging_and_s3_pocket_starts_plan` plans from that
exact position and from (0.15, 10.0) to every free test goal (status SUCCEEDED,
>5 poses).

### Re-enabling RPP collision detection (A)

Harness `tools/nav2_tune.py` (v4, 2 chains per scenario, loaded machine,
`artifacts/nav2-m7-tuning/after-red-team/*.json`), corrected footprint:

| variant | scenario | all reached | cruise / incl-startup (m/s) | note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| shipped (detection **off**) | obstacle_field | yes | 2.46 / 2.35 | one chain brushed an obstacle (min clearance 0.01 m, flag threshold 0.02) |
| detection **on** | obstacle_field | **no** | 2.05 / 1.53 | both chains aborted, 61x "detected collision ahead", "Controller patience exceeded"; one chain never moved although the nearest obstacle was 0.78 m away |
| detection on + cost-regulated scaling | obstacle_field | **no** | 2.03 / 1.52 | same aborts |
| shipped | wall_start | yes | 1.90 / 1.62 | |
| detection on | wall_start | **no** | 2.12 / 1.46 | aborted / body-wall contact flagged |
| detection on + cost-regulated | wall_start | yes | 1.90 / 1.62 | within noise of shipped |
| shipped / on / on+cost | diamond | yes / yes / yes | 2.35 / 2.35 / 2.34 | |

**Decision: collision detection stays off** (and cost-regulated scaling stays
off; it was only tried together with detection, not alone). Cause (inference
from the aborts, not isolated further): RPP checks the arc to the look-ahead
point against *lethal* cells; with the conservative circle rasteriser lethal
cells extend up to ~0.07 m beyond the real surface, and navfn paths pass
within a few cm of obstacles, so the arc at up to 3.6 m/s clips them. The
consequence stays as documented: the controller cannot stop itself for a
boid; boids are handled by 2 Hz global replanning (boids are in the global
costmap, verified by the integration test) plus the reactive blend. The local
costmap therefore has no effect on the command.

### Speed before / after the footprint fix (same harness, shipped yaml)

"Before" = `runs/final_shipped.json` (radius 0.22, inside border, margin 0.3;
earlier session, also a loaded machine). "After" = shipped now. 2 chains per
scenario each, so +-0.1-0.2 m/s is noise.

| scenario | cruise before -> after | incl-startup before -> after |
|---|---|---|
| obstacle_field (maze) | 2.20 -> 2.46 | 2.10 -> 2.35 |
| wall_start | 1.86 -> 1.90 | 1.60 -> 1.62 |
| diamond | 2.52 -> 2.35 | 2.58 -> 2.29 |

### Controller speed cap in the real game (E)

`target_controller` clamps the Nav2 command to `speed`, which is
`agent_max_speed` (2.0 m/s) unless a boid is within `panic_distance` (6 m),
then `v_max` (3.6). This is the existing **stamina design** ("sprint only
under threat so stamina regenerates", comment in `target_controller_node.py`,
stamina drain 0.25/s, regen 0.35/s), not a bug, and it applies equally to the
reactive evader. So the cruise numbers above (2.2-3.2 m/s, measured with
stamina off and no cap) are a *controller capability*; the speed the target
actually has in the game is capped at 2.0 m/s whenever no boid is within 6 m,
and the sanity table reports that "in-game" speed instead.

### What is still open after this revision

- The maze stays below 2.5 incl-startup: it is now 2.35 (was 2.10); diamond dropped to 2.29 (was 2.58).
- Wall start stays at ~1.6 incl-startup (pivot-bound).
- The controller does not avoid boids itself (collision detection stays off).
- With boids present the target can still get stuck in the wall pocket
  (sanity repeat 2, seed 6: 15 plan failures from one start); see
  `artifacts/nav2-m7-sanity/README.md`.
